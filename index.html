No wrong answers here — a sentence or two per question is plenty. Skip any that don't apply.

Example — Penicillin: Smoke hits before you even taste it. Bright lemon up front, honey-ginger softening it, peat lingering underneath like a bitter anchor. Silky texture, no real weight. The hook is the smoke — it's what people describe to a friend afterward. Makes smoky Scotch feel approachable instead of intimidating, a good gateway pour. Pairing takeaway: echo the smoke, don't fight it — smoked fish, aged cheddar, char-grilled anything.
